FAQ

What do you do?

I audit and optimize e-commerce operations: landing pages, CRO, GA4/GTM tracking, technical SEO and AI-assisted catalogue tools. I do this daily in-house and on retainer for smaller stores.

Which platforms do you work on?

Shopify and WordPress for my own projects, plus whatever CMS a client already runs — in my day-to-day work that is an in-house CMS for landing pages and product content.

How does a catalogue audit work?

I run product data through rule-based checks for missing fields, inconsistent attributes and broken links, then rank issues by commercial impact. My own Catalogue Health Checker tool runs entirely in the browser and has been tested with sample catalogues of up to 20,000 products.

What does a landing-page check cover?

Content and translation, metadata, links and UTMs, GTM/GA4 tracking, desktop and mobile rendering, and a live check after publishing — the same checklist I use in my own work.

How do you work remotely?

Async-first: I communicate in writing, document decisions and join calls when they add more than a message would. All my current client work is already fully remote.
